3.30 SSF Crafting Step-by-Step

1. Endgame Tri-Elemental Imperial Claw (ilvl 82+)
  1. Use a Tier-5 Fractured Imperial Claw (T1/T2 Flat Ele or Attack Speed) or clean ilvl 82+ base.
  2. Spam Essences of Wrath/Hatred/Anger until hitting a second T1–T3 flat elemental prefix.
  3. Bench-craft the 3rd flat elemental prefix (or Hits can't be Evaded).
  4. With 3 prefixes locked in, craft Prefixes Cannot Be Changed (4 Divines + 1 Sacred Orb in 3.30) → Harvest Reforge Speed for T2+ Attack Speed → bench-craft Crit Chance/Multi.
2. 100% Spell Suppression Armour (Rog + Eldritch Currency)
  1. In Rog's window, pick Evasion/Armour bases starting with Spell Suppression, Life, or Chaos Res. Always take Upgrade modifier tiers; skip Remove lowest level mod if Suppression is lowest.
  2. For Tier-5 fractured bases or finished suffixes (Suppression + Chaos Res), apply 1 Lesser Ember + 1 Lesser Ichor, make Searing Exarch dominant, and roll prefixes with Eldritch Chaos/Exalt/Annul for T1 Life.
3. +1 Strike Gloves & Gold Gambling (Kirac / Faustus)
  • Gloves: Spam Lesser/Greater Eldritch Embers for Non-Vaal Strike Skills target 1 additional nearby Enemy + Ichors for Cold/Lightning Exposure on Hit.
  • Gold Management: Keep ~50k Gold buffer for Kirac's shop (uncompleted maps, Guardian maps, Expedition/Incursion Threads). Save the rest until Character Level 82–84+ to gamble Claws, Belts, and Amulets at Faustus's Black Market on ilvl 82–84 bases.

Fate Card Combos & Boss Rules

  • Sequential Drafting: You must pick a Fate at your current Cauldron before the next Cauldron reveals its cards. Clicking pauses the game so you can read safely.
  • 3-Card Cap & Duplicate Merging (3x Rewards!): Invoking two identical Fate Cards merges them into 1 slot, triples (3x) the rewards of the FIRST card played, and frees a slot for a 2- or 3-icon set combo.
  • Tier Upgrades: Merging two Tier 2 Fates upgrades them into a Tier 3 Fate.
  • Fated Gear Cards: Prioritize cards previewing a Fated reward (guaranteed Speed on boots or Crit/Speed on weapons).
  • Dangerous Fates for Melee LS: Watch out for Savage Fate (spawns a 2nd boss—pop Vaal LS + Unbound Avatar immediately) and overlapping ground degen (use instant Frostblink to cancel Multistrike locks).
