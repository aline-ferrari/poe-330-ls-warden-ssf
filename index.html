Start here: your next upgrade, one act at a time

This is a Ranger — Lightning Strike Warden SSF route for the normal campaign (not Ruthless). Begin with Frost Blades, then switch when Lightning Strike feels better. All life and gear targets below are practical recommendations, not quest requirements or guarantees; stop to fix a weak defense if dying repeatedly.

Your Act 1 priorities: take Frost Blades, get the Quicksilver Flask, use a fast weapon with a shield, find a useful 3-link, and collect the two early passive books. Keep a mana flask. You do not need a unique item, a perfect claw or a tincture yet.

Reward vs purchase: “Take” means a free quest selection; “buy” means vendor currency. Choose only one reward per selection, then buy any missing alternatives later. Random rare rewards are not guaranteed upgrades. Consume passive books immediately. The existing passive map remains a 3.29 reference; this page does not claim a verified 3.30 tree.

Check quests after claiming their rewards. Your checks save in this browser.
Act 1 — Lioneye's Watch — Merveil

Level 1–13 — Stay with Frost Blades while learning the game

Character checkpoint

About 300–450 life at the end of the act. Prioritize cold resistance for Merveil (aim for 50–75%); fire resistance helps against early fire enemies. Wear a shield, any life/resistance gear and movement-speed boots if found.

Skills & combat

Frost Blades + Momentum at first; add Added Cold Damage when available. A useful 3-link beats an unrelated rare item. Keep a life flask, mana flask and Quicksilver. Level Lightning Strike in spare sockets; do not force an early swap.

Craft now / save for later

Check Tarkleigh for a faster weapon and useful 3-links when you return to town. Use one low-tier elemental essence on a normal claw if your weapon is weak. Otherwise use a Transmutation on a new fast base; do not repeatedly reroll it. Buy a Sapphire Ring for Merveil if needed.

Take Dash for movement. Buy Sniper's Mark from Nessa after its unlock if affordable, for manual boss use. Keep attacking while Dash recharges.

Act 2 — Bandits, Herald and first support upgrades

Level 13–23 — Keep a strong 3-link

Character checkpoint

About 550–750 life. Aim for roughly 50% or more elemental resistances, improving toward 75%. Keep two current life flasks if needed, one mana flask and Quicksilver. Do not abandon life gear for a few extra damage stats.

Skills & combat

Frost Blades + Added Cold + Elemental Damage with Attacks is a simple option. Add Herald of Ice only if enough mana remains. LS is optional here: try LS + Added Cold + Ancestral Call/Pierce when available. Extra strike targeting and pierce improve clear; Multishot alone does not give either.

Craft now / save for later

Unlock your hideout through Helena; use its crafting bench for a missing resistance or life roll. Life crafts use a prefix; resistances use suffixes. Use cheap crafts only. Craft another claw only when damage slows down, not just because you reached the next act.

Act 3 — Library access and first Warden ascendancy

Level 23–34 — Aim for a useful 4-link

Character checkpoint

About 850–1,100 life. Bring elemental resistances close to 75%, especially lightning for Dominus. A 4-link with correct colors is more valuable than forcing a high-defense unlinked chest. Aim for good attack accuracy; use Precision or flat accuracy if missing often.

Skills & combat

Try LS + Added Cold + Elemental Damage with Attacks + Ancestral Call/Pierce. Once the additional-strike mastery is allocated, replace Ancestral Call. Use Trinity only when all three resonance bars fill during combat; use Nightblade once crits reliably grant Elusive.

Craft now / save for later

Bench-craft the weakest resistance and one useful claw stat if an affix slot is open. Use the tooltip's —Adds X to Y — Damage— on the weapon itself: added damage to spells does not improve your attack. Save Fusings; buy or loot a 4-link instead of chasing one.

Act 4 — Finish the campaign gem toolkit

Level 34–41 — Keep leveling your main gems

Character checkpoint

About 1,200–1,500 life with 75% elemental resistances where possible. Upgrade life flasks. A useful 4-link is enough; keep a fast claw and shield. Maintain a mana flask until leech/recovery actually sustains boss attacks.

Skills & combat

Continue your working 4-link. At level 38, test Returning Projectiles or Multistrike rather than adding an extra mana multiplier blindly. If Nightblade rarely grants Elusive, keep a reliable flat-damage or attack-speed support instead.

Craft now / save for later

Use low-tier essences on a spare claw, compare it with your equipped claw, and bench the missing flat elemental damage or attack speed when unlocked. Upgrade only if real damage/feel improves. Never overwrite your only usable weapon.

Act 5 — Prepare for the first resistance penalty

Level 41–46 — Check gear before Kitava

Character checkpoint

About 1,500–1,800 life. Aim for 75% elemental resistances after Kitava: you need 30 percentage points of headroom before the penalty, or gear/crafts ready to replace the loss. Keep movement and a guard skill available.

Skills & combat

A working LS 4-link, enough accuracy to connect, and mana for repeated boss attacks. Keep a mana flask if necessary; instant-leech mastery alone does not guarantee enough leech. Do not race through dangerous packs with uncapped resistances.

Craft now / save for later

Save resistance rings, belts and shields before the fight. Bench one missing resistance per useful open suffix. Upgrade life on armor only where an open prefix exists. Do not spend expensive currency on soon-to-be-replaced campaign gear.

Act 6 — Repair resistances and unlock Lilly's gems

Level 46–51 — Fix defenses before pushing

Character checkpoint

About 1,800–2,100 life. Restore displayed fire/cold/lightning resistance to 75% after the Act 5 penalty. Chaos resistance can improve gradually. Take a bleed-removal life flask when you can roll one; do not ignore burning ground or bleed.

Skills & combat

Lilly unlocks most normal gems. Fill missing movement, guard and attack supports. A low-level Precision, one major aura or Herald, and enough unreserved mana is a safe starting point. Blood Rage is optional until recovery offsets degeneration.

Craft now / save for later

First spend cheap bench currency on resistance gaps. Use a few Alterations on a spare flask for a useful recovery/immunity modifier, within what that flask level can roll. Keep a functioning life flask equipped during crafting.

Act 7 — Second Lab and three passive quests

Level 51–56 — Start the tincture setup deliberately

Character checkpoint

About 2,100–2,400 life and 75% elemental resistances. Improve life on every non-weapon slot. Suppression helps as you find it, but is not a substitute for resistance caps or life. Upgrade your flasks to usable current bases.

Skills & combat

Keep the 4-link. After Cruel Lab, test one Prismatic Tincture if found. Use it for tough packs/bosses, watch Mana Burn and deactivate it before mana collapses. You still need to press it again after cooldown.

Craft now / save for later

Roll only a spare tincture with a few Alterations; one useful modifier is enough during the acts. Prioritize the claw, life and resistances over perfect tincture affixes. Keep your mana flask until a sustained boss test proves it is unnecessary.

Character checkpoint

About 3,000–3,500 life. Prepare to keep 75% elemental resistances after another −30% penalty (−60% total from both Kitava fights). You can check the parenthesized uncapped value: aim for at least 105% in Act 10 before the fight.

Acquisition cheat sheet

  • Act 1: Frost Blades from Tarkleigh after Hillock; Added Cold from Nessa after Brutus; normal Lightning Strike from Nessa's Siren's Cadence reward, usable at level 12.
  • Act 2, Sharp and Cruel: take Elemental Damage with Attacks; buy Trinity and Nightblade from Yeena. Nightblade is a vendor purchase for Ranger, not her free reward.
  • Act 3: Siosa after A Fixture of Fate sells many earlier gems across classes. Buy missing utility and clear supports here; do not expect Act 4 level-38 supports.
  • Act 4: Returning Projectiles and Multistrike unlock through The Eternal Nightmare rewards/vendors; both need level 38.
  • Act 6: Lilly after Fallen from Grace fills almost all remaining normal-gem gaps. Vaal gems must be found or obtained by corruption; corrupt spare gems, never your only leveled main attack.

Sources: Lightning Strike, Nightblade, gem rewards.

Movement, auras and survival

  • Movement: Dash early. With a claw, use Whirling Blades + Faster Attacks; add Lifetap only if life recovery is sufficient. A separate Frostblink can cross gaps after you can buy it. Do not link every utility skill together just because sockets are linked.
  • Reservation: low-level Precision for accuracy, Herald of Ice while leveling, then Grace or one damage aura as your gear allows. Grace plus Wrath/Anger reserves 100% before efficiency; leave enough mana for several attacks and movement. Do not put Arrogance on major auras in this life build.
  • Guard: manually use Steelskin once acquired; later Steelskin + Automation is an option. Keep its Strength requirement manageable. Automation supports appropriate instant spells; it does not automate Ancestral Cry or Blood Rage.
  • Marks: manually cast Sniper's Mark on tough enemies initially. Later Mark on Hit + Sniper's Mark automates eligible hits at extra cost; add Lifetap only if the life cost is safe. Do not reserve so much mana that mark triggers prevent attacks.
  • Blood Rage: optional attack speed/Frenzy generation while clearing, with physical degeneration. Add it only when life recovery handles it; turn it off for fights without safe recovery. It does not solve single-target Frenzy generation on its own.
  • Flasks: keep life + mana + Quicksilver through early acts. Move toward bleed-removal life recovery and useful defensive utility (Jade/Granite/Quartz as available). Diamond helps crits. A tincture consumes a flask slot; never drop your only reliable life recovery for it.
  • Accuracy: aim for roughly 95–100% chance to hit against the content you are doing. Add flat accuracy/Precision when needed. Tooltip DPS alone does not establish boss readiness.
Trinity check: its three resonance bars should fill while hitting enemies. You need different elements to take turns dealing the highest damage in individual hits. Three elemental prefixes or a particular aura combination alone does not guarantee this. Do not use Elemental Focus if relying on freeze/scorch.
Ascendancy reference: PoB 3.29. Follow one coherent eight-point plan. Oath of the Magi is not in this Warden tree. Seasoned Hunter extends tinctures to ranged weapons and is unnecessary for claw Lightning Strike.

Suggested four-Lab route

Each step costs two ascendancy points including its small node. Eternal Lab requires level 68 and an Offering to the Goddess; finish it when comfortable in maps. Avatar of the Wilds is an alternative plan to evaluate in PoB, not an extra notable you can add to these eight points.

PoB 3.29 tree source checked for notable effects and the Enduring Suffusion — Experienced Herbalist connection. Internal ascendancy identifiers may retain the older —Raider— name.

Tinctures: use them without losing your mana

  1. First find a useful base. Prismatic supports elemental melee damage; Rosethorn helps melee critical strikes. They are optional during early acts. Apply a small budget of Alterations to a spare base; stop at one useful effect/attack-speed/penetration roll rather than chasing perfect tiers.
  2. Activate manually for hard enemies. Watch the Mana Burn counter and your available mana. Tinctures periodically consume mana through Mana Burn; the first-six-stacks mastery helps but does not eliminate later drain.
  3. Build toward the two mastery choices. At Practised Reapplication, choose “The first 6 Mana Burn applied to you have no effect”; at Burst of Energy, choose deactivation at 12 or more Mana Burn. The mastery nodes must be allocated and their effects selected in game.
  4. Lingering effects are conditional. Enduring Suffusion gives 0.5 seconds per stack at deactivation, capped at six seconds. Earlier deactivation means less linger. Mana Burn past six stacks still matters; test against a boss before reserving more mana.
  5. Reactivate after cooldown. The mastery automatically deactivates a tincture; it does not press the button again. Keep a mana flask until attacks, marks and tinctures are sustainable together. Disable a tincture if your mana empties.
Do not delay campaign progress to complete both tincture wheels. First fix life, resistances, weapon damage and accuracy. Tincture uptime depends on your actual cooldown, Mana Burn rate and recovery.

Loot-filter colors and installation

The LS_Warden_330_SSF.filter in this repository is the single filter to copy. Equipment text keeps its rarity color even when it has useful links or is a claw base.

RARE — yellow text
MAGIC — blue text
NORMAL — white text
UNIQUE — orange + star
4-LINK — purple border
CURRENCY — bronze/gold

Extra borders: cyan = claw candidate; purple = 4-link; pink = 5/6-link; green = compact RGB recipe. A yellow-text item with a special border is still rare. Sounds/beams are reserved mainly for valuable drops, not every ordinary rare.

  1. In PoE open Options — Game — Item Filter and use the folder button to open the actual filter directory (often Documents/My Games/Path of Exile; OneDrive may redirect it).
  2. Copy the edited LS_Warden_330_SSF.filter from this repository into that directory. Replace the previous copy with the same name.
  3. Select that filter in the dropdown and press Reload / save. The game must report a successful load; an old loaded filter will not update just because the repository changed.
  4. In an Act 1 area, compare normal, magic and rare equipment on the ground. Normal is white, magic blue, rare yellow. All ordinary equipment stays visible through area level 32. Hold the game's show-all-items key if checking a hidden item later.

The filter keeps all rares, uniques, currency, gems, maps and major progression drops visible. Later it hides lower-value normal/magic equipment after useful bases, links and recipes have been handled. It does not decide whether an unidentified rare has good affixes.

Simple SSF Atlas plan

Unwavering Vision is optional: its extra points come with a scarab restriction. Read the full current tooltip and choose whether that tradeoff suits your strategy. Skip cluster-jewel sockets until the jewel and travel cost actually improve your character.

Upgrades you can earn without a required unique

  • First: a better fast claw, life/resistance rares, a useful 5/6-link, accuracy, suppression and reliable flasks. Keep a spare weapon for crafting.
  • Then: suitable Eldritch implicits, strong jewels in efficient nearby sockets, and damage/crit upgrades that preserve defenses. Confirm exact implicits before refunding strike or pierce passives.
  • The Taming: a possible later upgrade if you find Berek's Grip, Berek's Pass and Berek's Respite for its vendor recipe. Do not expect it from an ordinary resistance-ring craft or a guaranteed Harvest unique swap.
  • Timeless/cluster jewels: optional later projects. Check the actual seed/effects and total passive cost in PoB; a named jewel is not automatically an upgrade.

If you are stuck

Dying: remove dangerous map mods, restore caps and life, upgrade recovery, then assess suppression/ailments. Low damage: check links, accuracy, weapon attack damage, Trinity resonance and Elusive before spending more currency. No mana: reduce reservation, keep a mana flask, disable tinctures, then test leech and trigger costs against a boss.
