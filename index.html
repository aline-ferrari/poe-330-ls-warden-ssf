Neon Green Drop
  1. Pick up ilvl 82+ Imperial Claws (or Tier-5 Fractured Imperial/Gemini Claws with T1/T2 Flat Ele or Attack Speed).
  2. Spam Essence of Wrath / Anger / Hatred until hitting a second T1–T3 flat elemental prefix + an open prefix and open suffix.
  3. Bench-craft the 3rd Flat Elemental Damage prefix (or Hits Can't Be Evaded veiled/bench craft if accuracy isn't capped).
  4. Bench-craft Attack Speed or Critical Strike Chance on the open suffix.
Electric Cyan Drop
  1. Grab ilvl 84+ Dragonscale Gauntlets or Slink Gloves.
  2. Spam Essence of Greed (Life) or Essence of Zeal (Attack Speed) until hitting 12–14% Spell Suppression + Life/Res.
  3. Spam Lesser / Greater Eldritch Embers (Searing Exarch) for "Non-Vaal Strike Skills target 1 additional nearby Enemy" (allows unspeccing the Attack Mastery point!).
  4. Roll Eldritch Ichors (Eater of Worlds) for Cold or Lightning Exposure on Hit.
Electric Cyan Drop
  1. Use Full Dragonscale / Zodiac Leather (Body), Dragonscale / Slink Boots, and Royal Burgonet / Lion Pelt (ilvl 84+ for T1 Suppression).
  2. Spam Essence of Greed (Life) or Essence of Envy (Chaos Res) until hitting T1–T2 Spell Suppression + open prefix for bench Life/MS.
  3. Roll Eldritch Implicits: Aura Effect / +Max Res (Body), Action Speed / Ailment Avoid (Boots), Mana Reservation Efficiency (Helmet).
Neon Green Drop
  1. Pick up Prismatic Tinctures (Elemental Damage) and Rosethorn Tinctures (Crit Chance) highlighted in Neon Green.
  2. Scour → Transmute → spam Orb of Alteration + Orb of Augmentation for Increased Tincture Effect + Elemental Penetration, Attack Speed, or Critical Strike Multiplier.

3-Stage Auto-Scaling Currency & Gear Strictness (Automatic by AreaLevel)

Stage 1: Campaign & White/Yellow Maps (AreaLevel ≤ 77)

Shows all bubble-gum currency (Scrolls, Transmutes, Augs, Scraps, Whetstones), all Alts/Chroms/Jeweller's, and all Tier 3–4 build bases.

Stage 2: Red Map Strict (AreaLevel 78–82)

Automatically hides Scrolls, Transmutes, Augs, Scraps & Whetstones; restricts Tier 3–4 build gear & utility flasks to ilvl 84+ (ilvl 82+ for Claws).

Stage 3: T16 Uber-Strict (AreaLevel ≥ 83)

Shows Alterations, Chromatics & Jeweller's Orbs only in stacks of 3+, hiding single drops so T16 maps stay razor-clean.
